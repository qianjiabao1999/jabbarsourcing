Yiwu Sourcing Guide: Process, Pricing & Inspection

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Yiwu hosts the world’s largest small-commodity market, and sourcing there is straightforward once you know the process. This guide explains, step by step, how Jabbar Sourcing Team — the international sourcing team of Zhejiang Haoduobao Brand Management Co., Ltd., operating since 2021 with a 300+ person team, a 50,000 m² warehouse and shipments to 100+ countries and regions — takes an order from first inquiry to delivered goods.

The sourcing process, step by step

1. Send requirements. Share your product, quantity, target market and budget through the inquiry form or WhatsApp. Reference links or photos help us match faster.

2. Match suppliers. We screen Yiwu suppliers and prepare quotation lists. You receive a complete quotation within 24–48 hours; for complex multi-SKU projects we confirm requirements within 24 hours first.

3. Inspect goods. A dedicated specialist follows the order, opens cartons and packages one by one, and sends photos and videos in real time for your confirmation before packing.

4. Warehouse and ship. We arrange warehousing, consolidation and international shipping, and keep you updated until the goods arrive.

Pricing and order thresholds

We do not add a separate buyer commission. Each quote clearly lists product, inspection, warehousing, packing and logistics costs, so you always see where every dollar goes.

First cooperation supports trial orders from USD 1,000; regular orders start from USD 3,000 and support mixed-category wholesale. Consolidated container orders start from USD 10,000.

Payment methods: T/T bank transfers to a USD account, Alipay, and Alibaba.com Pay. We focus on wholesale sourcing consolidation and FCL/LCL shipping, and do not provide one-piece dropshipping.

Quality inspection

Every order is inspected before it ships. Your specialist unpacks cartons one by one, checks the goods against the confirmed samples or specifications, photographs and films the result, and waits for your confirmation before packing. Problems found at this stage are resolved with the supplier before the goods enter consolidation.

Consolidation and container shipping

Goods from different suppliers are consolidated in our Yiwu warehouse and loaded together. As a planning rule of thumb we fill a 20GP with about 28 CBM, a 40GP with about 58 CBM and a 40HQ with about 68 CBM, keeping roughly a 5% reserve for safe loading. To estimate your own shipment, try the container load calculator.
